NAKIBUULE COFFEE VENTURES
Business Plan Summary
November 2024

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
===============
Nakibuule Coffee Ventures is a specialty coffee aggregation and processing company focused on premium Arabica beans from Ugandan smallholder farmers. We provide comprehensive quality management, full supply chain traceability, and direct market linkage to international buyers.

COMPANY OVERVIEW
===============
Established: 2012
Location: Kampala, Uganda
Business Registration: UG-BIZ-2023-0001
Employees: 25 permanent, 8 seasonal
Annual Turnover: $450,000 (2023)

BUSINESS MODEL
===============
1. Farmer Partnerships: Direct relationships with 850+ smallholder farmers
2. Quality Control: Wet and dry processing facilities in Kampala
3. Certification: UTZ certified, working toward Fair Trade certification
4. Export: Direct B2B sales to specialty coffee retailers and roasters

MARKET FOCUS
===============
Primary Markets: USA, UK, EU, Canada
Product Lines:
- Single-origin Arabica (Fort Portal origin)
- Co-mingled specialty lots
- Specialty flavored coffee products
- Coffee equipment and training

GROWTH STRATEGY (2024-2026)
==========================
Year 1:
- Expand to Mubende and Kasese districts
- Increase farmer network to 1,500
- Build processing capacity to 200 MT/year

Year 2:
- Establish dedicated export processing facility
- Launch own retail brand
- Train 500 additional farmers in quality standards

Year 3:
- Open regional hub in Nairobi
- Develop cold-chain logistics network
- Achieve Fair Trade certification across all supply chain

INVESTMENT REQUIREMENTS
=======================
Total Capital Needed: $150,000
- Processing equipment: $60,000
- Export facility setup: $50,000
- Working capital: $40,000

Expected ROI: 25% annually

COMPETITIVE ADVANTAGES
=====================
1. Direct farmer relationships eliminate middlemen
2. Full traceability from farm to cup
3. Certified sustainable practices
4. Strong international buyer network
5. Local expertise combined with global standards
